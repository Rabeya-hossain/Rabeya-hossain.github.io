Rabeya Hossain.

PhD student / she/her

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Utah, advised by Prof. El Kindi Rezig. My current research is part of the CosmicAI project.

My work explores data quality and data preprocessing, including discovering label outliers in radio astronomy signals and parameter search for preprocessing pipelines.

Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My undergraduate research focused on instruction tuning for general-purpose Bengali language tasks. My teaching experience includes computer science courses at United International University.

Outside research, I enjoy traveling, movies and series, and a good problem to solve.
