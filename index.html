Rabeya Hossain

Hi, I’m Rabeya! I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Utah, working with Prof. El Kindi Rezig. My current research is part of the CosmicAI project. I work on data quality and data preprocessing, including label outliers in radio astronomy signals and parameter search for preprocessing pipelines.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My undergraduate thesis focused on instruction tuning for general-purpose Bengali language tasks, supervised by Dr. Rifat Shahriyar. My teaching experience includes computer science courses at United International University.

Outside research, I enjoy traveling, watching movies and series, and problem-solving.

Find me on LinkedIn and GitHub, or email me at rabeya.hossain@utah.edu.
