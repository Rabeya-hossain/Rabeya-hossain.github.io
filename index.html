Rabeya Hossain

Hi, I’m Rabeya! I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Utah, working with Prof. El Kindi Rezig. My research focuses on data quality and data preprocessing, including detecting label outliers in radio astronomy signals and tuning preprocessing pipelines. I am currently working on the CosmicAI project.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My undergraduate thesis focused on instruction tuning for general-purpose Bengali language tasks, supervised by Dr. Rifat Shahriyar.

Outside research, I enjoy traveling, watching movies and series, and problem-solving.

Find me on LinkedIn and GitHub. Email: rabeya.hossain@utah.edu.
